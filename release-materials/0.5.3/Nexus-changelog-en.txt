0.5.3 (test build)
- Added optional Keep open after equip for gear, spells, powers, shouts and actions; potions and food still close. Disabled by default.
- Added LT/RT left/right equip and neutral use. Choose LB/RB or LT/RT for categories; the other pair handles equip/use. A/X and D-Pad paging remain available.
- Separated Keyboard and Controller settings; updated localized labels and input hints.
- Retained actions refresh equipment state and keep native input isolation. Pause briefly resumes for processing, then restores; outfit jobs finish before pausing again.
- Includes 0.5.1 contextual-entry fixes and 0.5.2 GOG 1.6.1179 support.
