0.5.1
- Added GOG 1.6.1179 support (requires matching GOG SKSE64 and Address Library).
- Equipment, spells, powers, shouts and actions now keep the wheel open by default; potions and food still close. Configurable under Gameplay.
- Added controller LT/RT left/right equip and neutral use, with a choice of LB/RB or LT/RT for category switching.
- Separated Keyboard and Controller settings.
- Improved contextual hotkey compatibility and settings persistence across restarts; added clearer configuration diagnostics.
