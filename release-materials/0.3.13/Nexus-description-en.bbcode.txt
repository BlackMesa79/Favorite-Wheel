[center][size=6][b]Favorite Wheel - Radial Actions[/b][/size]
[color=#C9AA72]Your favorites and actions, at your fingertips.[/color]

A categorized favorites wheel and a separate home for quick actions.[/center]

Replace Skyrim's Favorites menu with a mouse-driven radial interface. Browse your favorite items and spells by category, check useful item information, and use a separate action wheel to change outfits or control optional Face Lighting features. Favorites and actions have their own pages, giving additional functions room to grow without crowding your item list.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Your favorites. Your actions.[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Categorized favorites[/b] — Weapons, equipment, potions, food, magic, and other items. Each page holds ten entries, with extra pages for larger collections. The wheel uses the favorites you already mark in your inventory and magic menus.
[*][b]A separate action wheel[/b] — Switch between favorites and functions inside the wheel, or open the action wheel directly. Outfit presets and optional Face Lighting controls occupy separate categories.
[*][b]Useful item details[/b] — Inspect available damage, armor, weight, value, spell cost, and effect or enchantment information while hovering an entry. Information depends on the item type and available game records.
[*][b]Outfit presets[/b] — Save your currently worn armor, clothing, and jewelry, then select a preset to change outfits. Rename, overwrite, delete, import, or export presets from the wheel.
[*][b]Optional Face Lighting controls[/b] — Toggle player lighting, control the NPC targeted before opening the wheel, and manage follower lighting through a compatible Face Lighting SKSE installation.
[*][b]A customizable interface[/b] — Original line icons, a compact category ribbon, diamond page indicators, hover details, and staggered fan-style opening and closing animations. Adjust wheel size, position, background dimming, sounds, and animations.
[*][b]Built-in settings[/b] — Configure the wheel in-game without SKSE Menu Framework. Includes English and Simplified Chinese, four themes, and editable language and theme files.
[*][b]Remembered categories[/b] — Favorites and actions independently remember their last category during the current game session. Changing categories keeps the wheel cursor in place.
[/list]

The action wheel is intended to accommodate additional functions as development continues. The current release includes outfit presets and the optional Face Lighting integration described below.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Keyboard & mouse[/b][/color][/size]
These are the default controls. The Favorites shortcut follows your game's binding unless overridden in the wheel settings.
[list]
[*][b]Q[/b] — Open or close the favorites wheel.
[*][b]Shift + Q[/b] — Open the action wheel directly.
[*][b]R[/b] — Switch between favorites and actions while the wheel is open. Configurable in settings.
[*][b]A / D[/b] or [b]Left / Right[/b] — Change categories.
[*][b]W / S[/b], [b]Up / Down[/b], or the [b]mouse wheel[/b] — Change pages within the current category.
[*][b]Mouse movement[/b] — Select an entry. The cursor stays inside the wheel; moving to the center clears the selection.
[*][b]Left click[/b] or [b]Enter[/b] — Use an item, equip to the right hand where applicable, or execute an action.
[*][b]Right click[/b] — Equip supported weapons, spells, or scrolls to the left hand; manage an outfit preset on the outfit page.
[*][b]Esc / Tab[/b] — Close the wheel. In the follower list, return to the Face Lighting page.
[*][b]F2[/b] — Open the built-in settings while the wheel is open.
[/list]

Opening the wheel pauses the game. A successful item or action selection closes it, then executes after gameplay resumes. Opening and closing animations are visual transitions and do not delay the actual action. Selecting a spell or shout equips it; cast or shout using the normal game controls.

Selecting an already equipped weapon in the corresponding hand, or an equipped armor item, unequips it. Ordinary keyboard and mouse input is supported; gamepad navigation is not included in this release.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Outfit presets[/b][/color][/size]
Wear the equipment you want to save, open the action wheel with [b]Shift + Q[/b], and choose [b]SAVE CURRENT[/b] on the outfit page. Enter a name and confirm.

[b]Left-click a preset[/b] to remove your currently worn managed equipment and then equip the saved outfit. If your current managed equipment exactly matches the preset, selecting it again removes that outfit instead.

[b]Weapons, shields, and ammunition are excluded.[/b] Presets manage armor, clothing, jewelry, and other non-shield armor items in your own inventory. Items do not need to be favorited. Presets do not create equipment or retrieve it from followers or containers.

[b]Right-click a preset[/b] to rename it, replace it with your current outfit, delete it, or export it. Overwrite and delete require confirmation. Deleting a preset does not delete its items.

[b]Save the game after editing presets.[/b] Presets are stored per character in the SKSE co-save. Loading an older save restores the presets from that save. Keep the matching [b].skse[/b] co-save when transferring saved games.

Exported [b].fwo[/b] files are written to [b]Data/SKSE/Plugins/FavoriteWheel/Outfits/Exports/[/b]. Place files in [b]Data/SKSE/Plugins/FavoriteWheel/Outfits/Imports/[/b] and select [b]IMPORT OUTFITS[/b] to import them. With Mod Organizer 2, new files may appear in Overwrite or your configured output mod.

Importing requires matching equipment in the destination character's inventory. It does not copy items. Presets containing dynamic forms or player-created dynamic enchantments cannot currently be exported. These files are specific to Favorite Wheel and are not Outfit Wheeler files.

Missing or ambiguous equipment prevents a change from starting. Improving, enchanting, or renaming saved items may require overwriting the preset. Protected quest equipment and shield-slot conflicts are checked. Equipment changes run in steps; another mod can block or alter a step, leaving a partial outfit change. The wheel reports incomplete changes rather than forcing restricted equipment off.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Optional integrations[/b][/color][/size]
[b]Face Lighting SKSE[/b]
Requires a build providing a compatible [b]public API V1[/b]. Compatibility is based on that interface, rather than an exact mod release number.

The Face Lighting category appears only when a compatible interface is detected. [b]Without Face Lighting, the category is hidden[/b] and favorites and outfits remain available. A detected interface that is temporarily not ready retains its category with a status message.

Aim at an NPC before opening the wheel to use the targeted-actor control. Follower lighting includes a group switch and an individual follower list. An enabled preference does not guarantee visible light: the lighting mod's perspective, sneaking, dialogue, and environment rules still apply. Detailed lighting configuration remains in Face Lighting's own settings.

[b]Skyrim Text Bridge[/b]
Compatible versions can provide IME input when naming presets. Use the input-method shortcut configured in Text Bridge. Without it, normal keyboard input and Unicode clipboard paste remain available. Neither integration is required for the core wheel.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Get started[/b][/color][/size]
Install with your mod manager, enable the mod, and launch through SKSE. No ESP, Papyrus scripts, or SWF files are required.

[b]Required[/b]
[list]
[*][url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url], matching your installed game version.
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url], matching your installed game version.
[*]Microsoft Visual C++ 2015–2022 x64 Redistributable.
[/list]

Mark items and spells as favorites in your usual inventory or magic menu, return to gameplay, and press [b]Q[/b]. The wheel only lists favorites; it does not automatically add your entire inventory.

Open the wheel and press [b]F2[/b] to configure it. Changes preview in-game; [b]APPLY[/b] saves them and returns to the wheel, while canceling discards unsaved changes. You can also edit [b]Data/SKSE/Plugins/FavoriteWheel.ini[/b] and restart the game. Set [b]Enabled=0[/b] to restore the original Favorites menu.

Keep your existing INI and custom language/theme files when upgrading. Mod Organizer users should also check Overwrite for settings written by the game. The supplied configuration starts in Simplified Chinese; choose [b]English[/b] in settings or set [b]Language=en[/b] in the INI.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Skyrim SE 1.5.97 — Tested in-game.[/b]
[*][b]Skyrim AE 1.6.1170 — Tested in-game.[/b]
[*][b]Skyrim 1.7.x — Supported, but not yet tested in-game.[/b] This build specifically supports [b]1.7.99 and 1.7.104[/b]; support does not automatically extend to every future 1.7.x update. Use the matching SKSE64 and Address Library, including the version-5 address database for these runtimes.
[/list]

Other runtimes are not supported by this build. Skyrim VR is not supported. Testing on the versions above does not certify every third-party mod combination.

[b]SkyUI[/b] is not required. Favorite entries come from the game's favorites data. Disable overlapping Favorites-menu replacement features in other mods when using this wheel.

[b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/97674]Ultimate Animated Potions NG[/url][/b] and [b][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/42602]Eating Animations and Sounds SE[/url][/b] worked in user testing. Consumables use the game's equipment path so those mods can receive their normal triggers. This is not a guarantee for every animation-mod setup.

[b]Community Shaders and other overlays[/b] may depend on render order and their specific versions. The wheel's dimming overlay is designed to cover the game's HUD; it does not certify every external overlay, HDR, ENB, or frame-generation combination. Background blur is not used.

[b]Inventory icon packs[/b] such as I4-based SWF libraries are not used. The wheel supplies its own line icons and needs no external icon dependency.

[b]Item details[/b] are practical information from game records and supported calculations, rather than a complete copy of the vanilla or SkyUI description card. Trading prices, conditional/scripted effects, and special mod-specific corrections may differ. Poison application is not implemented; use poisons through the inventory instead. Beast-form controls return to the original Favorites menu.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Themes & translations[/b][/color][/size]
Includes [b]Classic Gold, Frost, Engraved Gold, and Quiet Slate[/b], plus [b]English and Simplified Chinese[/b]. Select them in the built-in settings.

To create a translation, copy [b]SKSE/Plugins/FavoriteWheel/Languages/en.ini[/b] to a new language filename, keep the keys unchanged, and translate their values. Set a display name and save as UTF-8. Missing text falls back to English; item and spell names continue to come from the game.

To make a theme, copy an existing file in [b]SKSE/Plugins/FavoriteWheel/Themes/[/b] to a new filename. Themes support colors, font settings, ornament and relief strength, line widths, and local transition settings. Restart after adding files, then select them in settings. Use a font that supports your language. Theme image replacement and arbitrary layouts are not included.
